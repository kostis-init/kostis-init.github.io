About

I am currently pursuing a PhD at the DTAI lab of KU Leuven, focusing on the intersection of Constraint Programming and Large Language Models, under the supervision of Prof. Tias Guns. My research explores how large language models can support constraint modelling, from translating natural-language problem descriptions into symbolic models to improving their solver efficiency.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a software engineer in the industry, where I developed enterprise-level applications. I hold a Master's in Computer Science from KU Leuven, majoring in AI, and a Bachelor's in Informatics & Telecommunications from NKUA, majoring in CS.
